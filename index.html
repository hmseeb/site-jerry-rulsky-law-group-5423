FAQ

Questions we hear every day

How much does it cost to hire your firm?

Nothing upfront. Injury and accident cases are handled on a contingency basis — you pay no attorney fees unless we recover money for you. If there is no recovery, you owe us nothing.

How long do I have to file a claim in California?

Most personal injury claims in California are subject to a two-year statute of limitations from the date of injury, though some claims are shorter. The safest thing to do is contact us as soon as possible so no deadline is missed.

What should I do right after an accident?

Get medical attention first, report the incident, and keep everything — photos, witness names, the police report number, medical bills and any correspondence from insurers. Then call us before you give a recorded statement to an insurance adjuster.

Should I talk to the insurance company on my own?

It's best not to give a recorded statement or sign a release before speaking with an attorney. Adjusters are trained to minimize payouts, and early statements are often used against injured people later.

Will my case go to court?

Many injury cases resolve through negotiation. Still, we prepare every case as if it may be tried, which strengthens your position and often produces better settlements.
